# Codebook

## `post_id`

Stable occurrence identifier.

## `source`

Blog series: World Bank Development Impact or Economics That Really Matters.

## `academic_season`

Job-market season, formatted YYYY-YY.

## `publication_date`

Date of the source blog post.

## `date_added_to_archive`

First public archive release date for the occurrence.

## `blog_post_title`

Title of the source blog post and public entry title.

## `blog_post_url`

Original blog-post URL.

## `featured_author`

Featured job market paper author.

## `job_market_institution`

Institution at the time of the job market post.

## `author_website`

Author website linked from the source post, when available.

## `paper_title`

Reviewed paper title, when available.

## `paper_url`

Single useful paper link checked as of August 2026, when available.

## `paper_url_checked_date`

Date associated with the current paper-link check.

## `geographies`

Reviewed study geographies; multiple values separated by ` | `.

## `research_methods`

Reviewed research methods; multiple values separated by ` | `.

## `research_topics`

Reviewed topics; multiple values separated by ` | `.

## `units_of_analysis`

Reviewed units of analysis; multiple values separated by ` | `.

## `study_populations`

Reviewed populations of interest; multiple values separated by ` | `.
