# Methodology

The archive covers job market paper posts published by the World Bank's Development Impact blog and Economics That Really Matters. It is not a census of development economics job market papers.

The unit of observation is the blog-post occurrence. Cross-posts are retained separately because they are separate source publications.

Academic seasons use the job-market convention `YYYY-YY`. Source-visible season language, sequence language, and annual indexes are used where available.

The original blog post is the archive's primary source record. Paper links are provided as a convenience and were last checked in August 2026. They may point to a working-paper or published version and may change or disappear over time.

Paper titles are included when supported by reviewed paper evidence. The blog-post title remains the archive entry title.

Classifications cover geography, research method, research topic, unit of analysis, and study population. They were prepared with LLM assistance and human review; they are not mechanically inferred keywords.

Country geography tags are added only when the featured blog post clearly identifies those countries as study settings. Multi-country is used for studies spanning multiple countries that are not genuinely global and may include concise scope detail. Named regional tags describe actual study scope. Global scope is reserved for worldwide evidence.

The static country choropleth uses Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 country boundaries, which are public domain.

Known source limitation: for the World Bank 2016-17 season, source-visible evidence identified 16 listed posts while an official World Bank page stated that 17 were expected. The archive includes the source-supported records and does not infer an unidentified missing post.

Future seasons can be appended by running the same source preservation, enrichment, review, and public-output process while retaining stable occurrence IDs.
